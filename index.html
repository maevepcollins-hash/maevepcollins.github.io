ABOUT
Hi, I'm Maeve! I’m a designer, artist, and New Jersey apologist based in New York City. I earned my BFA in Communication Design from Washington University in St. Louis, where I developed a love for creating work that bridges fine art and design. My practice spans print, digital, and environmental experiences, with a focus on thoughtful visual storytelling and tactile, engaging design. Check out my CV here.
