PRINT / BOOK ARTS
Three-ring binder mounted in custom silkscreen case with vinyl slip cover. Printed on 80lb white matte, metallic silver, and various G.F. Smith Colourplan stocks.
2025
Inspired by Lisa McGee’s Derry Girls, this book collects different perspectives on the tumultuous history of Northern Ireland. I focused on personal accounts taken in the wake of the Troubles, leading up to the 1998 Good Friday Agreement. With the book I aim to present an alternative to the widely understood narrative of the Troubles, one that emphasizes the ridiculousness of being a young person, especially in an fraught environment like 1990s Northern Ireland. The book is accompanied by a slide projector and includes photo slides of Belfast and Derry’s political murals, that can be projected on a wall to view at their full size.
Calling to the famous “Free Derry” corner, this book’s silkscreen cover uses black serif type on a white ground, which is then wrapped in a vinyl slip case. The book includes an self-conducted interview, three collected essays, a photo essay with commentary from several historical photographers working in the 1970s and 1980s, and a scholarly article discussing the legacy of Northern Ireland’s political murals.
