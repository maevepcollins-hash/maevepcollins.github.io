Coptic bound book with laser cut acrylic covers. Printed on 80lb glossy stock and Astrobright paper. 
2024
Using text from This American Life’s podcast episode “807: Eight Fights,” this book looks at the relationships and conflicts between a family living between Russia and Ukraine at the start Russia’s full scale invasion in 2022. Texts used also include Vladimir Putin’s war declaration speech and three social media speeches delivered by Volodymyr Zelenksyy, President of Ukraine.
The book is sewn with a coptic binding and laser cut covers made with fluorescent cast acrylic. November 20th, 2024, marked one thousand days since Russia launched its full scale invasion of Ukraine.
